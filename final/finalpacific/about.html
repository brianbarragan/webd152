Our Family

Welcome to Pacific Trails Resort! Our kind staff are dedicated to providing you a memorable and relaxing stay. We'll treat you like family and make sure all your needs are met during your stay! If you would like any accommodations be sure to contact us beforehand so we can provide these accommodations as soon as you get here.

Our Story

Pacific Trails Resort was founded in 2010 by the Barragan family. We wanted to create a place where people could enjoy the natural beauty of Big Sur while experiencing the comfort of luxury camping. Our yurts are designed to provide a unique and serene experience for all our guests.
